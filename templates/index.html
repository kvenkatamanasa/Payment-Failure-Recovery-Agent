About the Project

Turning payment failures into actionable recovery decisions.

Why Payment Recovery?

Payment failures can result in lost revenue, customer frustration, and unnecessary operational effort.

This project provides an automated recovery workflow that identifies the type of payment failure, determines its likely root cause, recommends an appropriate action, and records every decision in an audit trail.

The system is designed as an educational simulation of an AI-assisted payment recovery agent and does not process real financial transactions.

AI Diagnosis Identifies payment failure type and probable root cause.
Recovery Recommendation Suggests an appropriate action based on the failure.
Safety Guardrails Prevents unsafe automated actions and sends high-value transactions for manual review.
Audit Trail Maintains recovery decisions and actions for monitoring.

Key Insights

The system provides useful insights into payment failures and recovery operations.

01

Failure Diagnosis

Payment failures are classified into meaningful failure scenarios such as timeout, network error, insufficient balance, UPI failure and gateway errors.

02

Recovery Decisions

The agent recommends actions such as retrying a payment, requesting another payment method, correcting payment details or requesting authentication.

03

Risk Control

High-value transactions above the configured safety threshold are blocked from automatic recovery and sent for manual review.

04

Recovery Rate

Recovery performance can be monitored using recovered payments compared with total failed transactions.

05

Revenue Recovery

The dashboard tracks revenue recovered through successful simulated recovery actions.

06

Transparency

Every recovery decision is recorded through the audit trail, making the system easier to monitor and explain.
